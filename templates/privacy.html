Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Spendly is a personal expense tracking application, and we designed this policy to be as straightforward as the app itself.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, including:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and password
  • Expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as login times, needed to keep your account secure

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide solely to operate and improve Spendly, including to:

  • Display your expenses, categories, and summaries back to you
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Diagnose bugs and improve the reliability of the Service

We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored in Spendly's database and are only accessible through your authenticated account. We take reasonable measures to protect this data, but no method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers, such as hosting or email delivery services, solely to operate the Service, and those providers only process data as needed to perform those functions on our behalf.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
